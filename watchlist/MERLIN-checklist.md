## ⚠️ Watch-out Traps (things that look like triggers but aren't)

1. **"MERLIN announces AI partnership / MOU"** — MERLIN signs MOUs frequently. Wait for **signed lease** or **capex deployment** on a specific site. MOUs are pre-LOI, not LOI.
2. **"NTA revaluation up €0.50/share"** — This is a revaluation, not new value. The market knows it's coming. Move on the **size** of the revaluation, not the direction.
3. **"Q-on-Q DC revenue up 15%"** — Small base, lumpy Phase II delivery. Look at **trailing-4-quarter** DC revenue + **forward IT load** for the real signal.
4. **"Hyperscaler visits Extremadura / Iberia"** — Site visits happen for years before contracts. Look for **signed power offtake** or **land lease** with named counterparty.
5. **"MERLIN raises new debt at 3.0%"** — MERLIN has issued at < 3% historically. Spread compression is good but small magnitude. Watch for **change in tenor** or **size of issuance**.
