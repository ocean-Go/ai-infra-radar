## 2. Thesis (Why MERLIN)

### Core identity
> "Iberia's hyperscale data center platform in REIT clothing" — the **supply-side shell twin** of the European AI Factory loop.

MERLIN is **not** a generic European REIT, **not** a speculative land banker, **not** a hyperscaler itself. It is the **only listed Iberian platform** that owns the land, the grid connection, the licenses, and the dark-fiber ring that the AI Cloud operators (CoreWeave, IREN, Nebius, Microsoft, AWS) need to scale. The asset class is "powered, permitted, fiber-connected land" — the **middle step** between raw power (IREN/Solaria twin) and the GPU operator (CRWV twin).

### The five-step loop (visible vs. hidden)

```
Step 1: Hyperscaler / NCP signs lease on powered land    ✅ PARTIAL (3/4 Tier-1 SOQs passed; ARA 03 leased, ARA 02/01 under construction)
Step 2: MERLIN secures grid connection + license + fiber  ✅ VISIBLE  (400kV Redeia, dark fiber ring 5 sites, Edged partnership)
Step 3: CapEx deploys on shell + cooling + substation     🟡 ACTIVE  (Phase II ongoing, Phase III plan, €447M DC capex in 2025)
Step 4: Customer energizes, takes IT load                 🟡 ACTIVE  (MAD 01 + BCN 01 + ARA 03 live, ARA 02/01 + LIS 01-05 building)
Step 5: Rent → FFO → dividend → NTA revaluation           ❌ UNVERIFIED (DC = 10% of revenue today, target 65% by 2030)
```

**Five steps. Two-and-a-half verified. Two-and-a-half unverified.** Thesis hinges on Step 3 (Phase III capex commitment) AND Step 5 (DC revenue mix reaching 50%+ to justify NTA expansion multiple).

### Why this is hard to replicate

- **Powered land scarcity:** Iberian 400kV substations with multi-GW headroom are finite. Navalmoral and Valdecaballeros were decommissioned nuclear sites with 6x generation surplus vs. regional consumption — Redeia grid studies show no peer sites of comparable scale.
- **Permit moat:** Extremadura regional government protocol signed Jan 2025. Spanish/Portuguese permitting cycles are 24–36 months for greenfield DC. Competitor sites starting today won't energize until 2028–2029.
- **Iberian platform optionality:** MERLIN's existing DC platform (5 sites: Madrid, Barcelona, Bilbao-Arasur, Lisboa-VFX, Zaragoza-planned) forms an interconnected **dark-fiber ring** that is the only Iberian equivalent to the FLAP-D (Frankfurt-London-Amsterdam-Paris-Dublin) cluster that hyperscalers use in northern Europe.
- **NCP supplier status (per CMD 2026):** "Solid commercial relationship with all Tier-1 players. Proven AI infra supplier of NVIDIA Cloud Partners." Pass-rate: 3/4 Tier-1 hyperscaler SOQs.

⚠️ **Honest caveat:** MERLIN's DC platform is still small — DC = 10% of FY25 revenue (€56M of €565M). The "65% within 5 years" target is CEO Clemente's stated ambition (Nareit interview), not a contracted milestone. **The moat is not "having land" — it's "having powered, permitted, fiber-connected, hyperscaler-credible land"** — and that moat is currently being built, not yet monetized.

**Key adjustment vs. CRWV:** MERLIN's capital structure is **already investment-grade (BBB)** — there's no Layer 4 re-rating to capture. The multiple expansion story is **NTA expansion** (€15.36 → €22–25), not EV/EBITDA re-rating.

### Layer 4 — NTA Expansion (REIT-Equivalent of Asset Securitizability)

> The market is **under-pricing** MERLIN's transition from "European retail/logistics REIT" to "Iberian AI infrastructure platform." NTA re-rating is the proof point.

- **Why Layer 4 matters for REITs:** Standard European REIT trades at 0.8–1.0x NTA (discount). **Infrastructure-grade REIT (cell towers, fibre, powered land) trades at 1.2–1.5x NTA** (premium). This 0.3–0.5x NTA delta is the Layer 4 alpha.
- **Current multiple:** 0.99x NTA (€15.24 / €15.36) = market is pricing existing assets, not the AI option
- **Target multiple (Base case):** 1.25x NTA = 25% premium
- **Signal to monitor:** First Phase III capex commitment PR (capex deployed, not announced) + first hyperscaler LOI on Bilbao/Zaragoza capacity
