## 2. Thesis (Why CoreWeave)

### Core identity
> "AI Factory operator under NVIDIA's DSX standard" — the **demand-side anchor** of the European AI Factory loop.

CoreWeave is **not** a generic cloud company, **not** a pure data-center landlord, **not** an NVIDIA chip reseller. It is the **execution arm** that converts NVIDIA architecture into monetizable AI Cloud capacity at hyperscaler-grade scale.

### The five-step loop (visible vs. hidden)
```
Step 1: AI customer signs multi-year take-or-pay   ✅ VISIBLE  (backlog $99.4B)
Step 2: NVIDIA allocates GPUs + reference design   ✅ VISIBLE  (5GW 2030, $2B equity)
Step 3: CoreWeave secures powered land + shell      🟡 PARTIAL (US active, EU building)
Step 4: CapEx deploys + customer consumes          🟡 PARTIAL (Q1 2026 active MW growing)
Step 5: Revenue → EBITDA → debt service → equity   ❌ UNVERIFIED (margin still 1%)
```

**Five steps. Three verified. Two unverified.** Thesis hinges on Step 5 margin recovery AND Step 3 European buildout speed.

### Why this is hard to replicate
- **Capital structure lock-in:** DDTL 4.0 ($7.5B → $8.5B, investment-grade, non-recourse, GPU-collateralized) is first-of-its-kind. Competitors can't issue the same paper without equivalent asset structure.
- **NVIDIA strategic layer:** $2B @ $87.20 + 5GW roadmap + DSX alignment. NVIDIA doesn't write these checks for everyone.
- **Customer concentration quality:** Microsoft, OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic, Jane Street. Top-3 customers are all "compute demand > internal capacity" profiles — they have to outsource.

⚠️ **Honest caveat:** NCP category is contested. IREN, Nebius, Nscale, Lambda, Crusoe all compete. **The moat is not "having GPUs" — it's "having the financing structure to scale GPUs at 5GW pace."**
