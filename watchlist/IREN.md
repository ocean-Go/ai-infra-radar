## 2. Thesis (Why IREN)

### Core moat
> "Energized now" — not "announced GW pipeline."

IREN owns 1.4GW at Sweetwater (Phase 1) already energized to ERCOT grid (target: May 2026). Childress 750MW is operational and host to Microsoft $9.7B contract. The 2028 pipeline (Australia 800MW, Bundy) is **promise, not asset** — those compete on a different axis entirely.

### Why this is hard to replicate
The "developer engine" requires: site sourcing → grid interconnection → permitting → power procurement → construction → capex deployment. IREN has executed this loop repeatedly: Childress → Sweetwater → Kiowa → BC (Canada) → Spain → Australia.

⚠️ **Honest caveat:** This engine is **not IREN-exclusive** — WULF, CORZ, APLD, CIFR, HUT all share it. IREN's differentiator is **execution speed + top counterparties (Microsoft, NVIDIA)**.

### Counterparty anchor
- **Microsoft:** $9.7B (multi-year, Nov 2025)
- **NVIDIA:** $3.4B AI cloud contract + 5GW strategic partnership (May 2026) + 30M warrants @ $70 strike (up to $2.1B)
- **Total visible backlog:** $15.2B+

## 6. Why We Are NOT Adding Yet

**Decision discipline:**
1. **Conviction is thesis-level, not yet execution-level.** "Energized now" is the right framework, but IREN must **prove** they can fill the 1.4GW. Premature scaling = buying on narrative, not numbers.
2. **Sell-side dispersion is wide** ($70–$100 = 43% range). When PTs span this much, the right move is **wait for clarity**, not chase.
3. **Pilot exists to learn, not to win.** A $155.50 ticket gives us a seat at every IREN catalyst. That's the value of the position. Size up only when the thesis proves out.
