### 论点 1: NBIS-Bloom $2.6B 燃料电池交易不打破 thesis，反而佐证
> NBIS scrapped 原计划 gas turbine，签 10 年 $2.6B SOFC contract，Phase 1 = 328MW。

**我们的回应：**

- NBIS 不是"用钱砸出来" power，是"用钱买绕路"
- IREN 直接拥有 grid-connected + energized power → 比 NBIS 那个稀缺

### 论点 2: IREN 真护城河是"今天能开机的 GW"
- ✅ Sweetwater 1: 1.4GW May 2026 energized to ERCOT；700k+ liquid-cooled GPU capacity at full build
- ✅ Childress: 750MW operational，stage for MSFT $9.7B
- ✅ 2028 公告 vs. 2026 energized = 不同维度 (promise vs. asset)

### 论点 3: Development engine (5-step loop)
1. Site sourcing
2. Grid connection
3. Permitting
4. Power procurement & ops
5. Construction & energization
+ Capital deployment

**已重复跑过:** Childress → Sweetwater → Kiowa (OK) → BC (Canada) → Spain → Australia
**作者诚实承认:** 这个 engine 不是 IREN 独家，是 WULF/CORZ/APLD/CIFR/HUT 共同特质 ($70B+ 累计 AI/HPC 合同)

### 论点 4: 两个 honest caveats
1. Engine 是类别优势，不是 IREN 独家
2. Onsite generation (Bloom/SMR) 是 long-term wild card
   - Bloom 2.8GW Oracle + 1GW AEP off-grid
   - 可能 erode grid-connected scarcity premium

### 论点 5: Nvidia 选 Sweetwater = 印证
- 1.4GW energized + sitting empty = 最高价值
- 60MW cloud @ Childress ($3.4B) + $2.1B equity warrants
- 加上 MSFT $9.7B → NVDA-related backlog = $13B+

### 论点 6: 2026 winner = 闭环 hyperscaler deals first
**指标：**
- ① Sweetwater fill rate
- ② Operational MW ramp
- ③ Financing ability amid losses & dilution

### 3.4 现价 $62 隐含什么

**$62 = market pricing in ~600–700MW fill scenario.**

推算逻辑：
- EV (market cap + net debt) ≈ 当前市值 ~$18B
- 按 EV/ARR 22x → 隐含 forward ARR ~$0.8B (但实际 2026E ARR $3.4B+ 已签约)
- 更合理推算：$62 pricing in 2027 ARR = $2.5–3.0B
- 2027 ARR = ASP × MW → 如果 ASP $2M/MW-yr，则 fill rate = 1.25–1.5GW ← 这反而比 Macquarie 更乐观
- **或者 market 在 discount future dilution 风险**

**最可能解释:**
$62 = market 同时 pricing in：
1. 部分 fill 风险 (500–800MW)
2. 未来 dilution 风险 (capex 持续)
3. Execution risk (Mirantis 转型)

**$90 PT = best case 1.2GW 全 fill。** 现价距离 $90 = +45%，但需要 Sweetwater 验证。

## 4. NBIS-Bloom 交易 vs. IREN 路径

| 维度 | IREN | NBIS-Bloom |
|---|---|---|
| Power type | Grid-connected + energized | Behind-the-meter fuel cell |
| Asset ownership | IREN owns power | Bloom owns, NBIS buys output |
| Time to power | Already on (May 2026 for Sweetwater 1) | Phase 1 deployment TBD |
| Cost structure | Fixed capex, low opex | Long-term $2.6B / 10yr opex |
| Scalability | Limited by grid | Limited by fuel cell deployment |
| **Scarcity** | **Higher** (sunk + energized) | Lower (replicable but costly) |

**结论:** 同一稀缺资源，不同解法。IREN 那条路**更难被复制** (要 land + grid + 当地政府)。
